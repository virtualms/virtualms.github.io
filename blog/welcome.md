# Welcome to my new space!

This is my new **Blog / Portfolio** built entirely in pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, following the *Neo Brutalism* stylistic paradigm.

## Why this choice?

- **Zero dependencies:** No complex frameworks or build steps.
- **Speed:** All rendering is executed locally on the browser.
- **Personality:** A strong design, inspired by the origins of the web but with a modern touch.

### How does the blog section work?
I write an article in Markdown (exactly like this one), put it in the `blog/` folder, run a local script, and the article instantly becomes available and readable on the page!
